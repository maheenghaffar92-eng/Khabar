How it works

Three steps. The family installs nothing.

1

Admission

Reception saves one family number, with consent, and gives the patient a token.

2

Staff tap

Staff tap the new stage on the update panel, check the preview, and confirm.

3

WhatsApp / SMS

A message with token, stage and time reaches the family phone.

Why families and staff like it

One-tap updates

Four buttons. Nothing to type.

Works on any phone

WhatsApp first, SMS as backup.

Private by design

Token number only. Never the patient's name.

Calmer Help Desk

Families get answers without crowding the desk.
